## Финальный запрос

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one production bitmap game asset for ASHBOUND, a grounded dark medieval fantasy RPG. A humble hand-drawn local courtyard sketch on worn warm grey-brown parchment, wide landscape composition 1536x1024. Entire image is a flat sheet viewed straight down, no perspective page mockup, no hands, no tabletop, no props around it. Restrained charcoal and faded sepia ink, cross-hatching and small muted moss accents, elegant readable silhouettes like an old traveller's notebook; earthy sombre palette, not bright fantasy treasure map. CONTENT MUST MATCH this small existing game yard: a single timber-framed inn HOUSE in the upper LEFT quarter, a simple timber GATE in stone piers at the TOP CENTRE, a tall square timber WATCHTOWER with a stone base in the upper RIGHT quarter, ONE round roofed stone WELL near the CENTRE, a small pile of LOGS on the LEFT below the house, a single wooden practice DUMMY on the RIGHT below the tower. A dirt path runs from bottom centre toward the gate, passing just left of the well. Sparse low fences near the lower sides. Only sparse pines beyond the top boundary. No other buildings, no mountains/castles/rivers/villages, no people, no monsters. Make the 6 landmark drawings large and clearly separated, occupying the middle 85% of parchment. Gentle folds and lightly worn edges, enough contrast at mobile size. No letters, no words, no numbers, no runes, no labels, no grid, no UI, no bright markers, no player position, no watermark. A small simple four-point compass motif without letters in lower left is acceptable. The image will be displayed inside a localized game interface, so ALL readable text is supplied externally. This is a small practical sketch of ONE courtyard, not a world map.
